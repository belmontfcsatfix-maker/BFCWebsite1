About the club

Rooted in Donnybrook since 1990

"Passionate, committed, and driven — a club shaped by the people who show up for it."

Belmont FC began over three decades ago as a handful of local enthusiasts with a ball and a patch of grass. Today, we're a full community club competing in the Dublin District Schoolboy League, affiliated with the FAI, playing home matches on the grass pitches of Herbert Park.

Every jersey, training cone, and coaching hour is funded by the club itself and run entirely by volunteers — parents, past players, and neighbours who believe Donnybrook should always have a club to call its own.

Youth development

Character before the scoreline

Youth development sits at the heart of Belmont FC. We train players at every level from age four upward, with a curriculum focused as much on discipline and teamwork as it is on technique.

It's this long view — patient coaching over quick wins — that's turned the club into a fixture of the local community and a launchpad for a new generation of players.

  • 01 Age-graded training pathways from U7 through U16
  • 02 FAI-aligned coaching standards across every squad
  • 03 Emphasis on sportsmanship, discipline and teamwork
  • 04 Volunteer coaches drawn from the Belmont community
