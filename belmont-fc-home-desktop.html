About us

Passionate, committed and driven

Belmont FC, founded in 1990, is a soccer club rooted in Donnybrook, Dublin 4. Our home matches take place on the grass pitches of Herbert Park, where we proudly don the colours yellow and blue. We compete in the Dublin District Schoolboy League (DDSL), which is affiliated with the FAI.
